# Third-party notices

Keep original licence and attribution files with any redistributed dependency.

The locked direct runtime dependencies declare:

| Package | Locked version | Declared licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| better-sqlite3 | 13.0.3 | MIT |
| drizzle-orm | 0.45.2 | Apache-2.0 |
| mdb-reader | 3.2.0 | MIT |

The lockfile also declares MIT-0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC,
BlueOak-1.0.0, MPL-2.0, CC0-1.0 and compound MIT/Zlib or MIT/BSD licences,
among build, development and transitive packages. Versions and declarations
are recorded in `package-lock.json`; inspect actual installed licence files
before preparing a release. Electron, Chromium, SQLite and platform libraries
carry their own notices and distribution requirements. No dependency binaries
or `node_modules` are committed to the source repository.

Manufacturer names and catalogue identifiers identify supported integrations.
Obtain external data and drawings separately and retain their applicable terms.
